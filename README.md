# 🔎 ResearchRadar

## Autonomous Research & Competitor Intelligence Agent

ResearchRadar is an autonomous AI-powered intelligence agent designed to help organizations, startups, and research teams monitor emerging research, identify important developments, analyze competitive activity, and turn information into actionable strategic insights.

Instead of simply summarizing information, ResearchRadar investigates a user-defined technology or research area, dynamically uses relevant intelligence tools, verifies findings, detects strategic signals, and produces a final recommendation.

## 💡 Problem Statement

Organizations, startups, and research institutions operate in rapidly evolving and highly competitive environments.

Keeping track of research developments, scientific publications, competitor activities, and emerging technologies manually is time-consuming and can result in important signals being missed.

Traditional tools often focus on collecting or summarizing information. They may not explain:

- What changed?
- Why does it matter?
- What competitors are doing?
- What emerging research should be monitored?
- What strategic action should be taken?

ResearchRadar addresses this problem by providing an autonomous intelligence workflow that moves from information discovery to strategic decision-making.

---

# 🚀 Solution

ResearchRadar acts as an autonomous research and competitive intelligence agent.

The user provides:

- A research or technology area
- Relevant competitors

The agent then:

1. Understands the monitoring objective
2. Creates an investigation plan
3. Dynamically selects relevant intelligence tools
4. Retrieves research and scholarly information
5. Cross-references findings
6. Detects important strategic signals
7. Tracks relevant organizations
8. Generates actionable recommendations
9. Produces a final strategic verdict
